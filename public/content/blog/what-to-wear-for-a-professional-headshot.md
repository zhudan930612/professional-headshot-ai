# What to Wear for a Professional Headshot

## 👔 Quick Headshot Outfit Rules
• Solid colors work better than patterns
• Navy, charcoal, and jewel tones are safest
• Proper fit matters more than brand
• Match outfit style to your industry
• Avoid pure black, neon colors, and visible logos

---

## The Power of First Impressions Through Clothing
What you wear in your professional headshot matters more than you think. Your clothing communicates professionalism, competence, and personal brand—often before anyone reads your resume or bio. The right outfit can enhance credibility and memorability, while the wrong one can quietly undermine your presentation.

💡 **Why this matters**
Headshots are trust signals. Clothing choices directly influence how trustworthy, capable, and approachable you appear.

---

## 🎨 Color Psychology: Choose Colors That Work for You

### ✅ Best Colors for Professional Headshots
- **Blues (navy, mid-blue)** – Conveys trust, stability, and professionalism; universally flattering
- **Jewel tones (emerald, sapphire, deep purple)** – Rich, confident, and camera-friendly
- **Warm neutrals (cream, tan, warm gray)** – Soft, approachable, ideal for education & healthcare

### ❌ Colors to Avoid in Headshots
- **Pure black** – Creates harsh shadows; can look severe
- **Neon / bright colors** – Distracting and unprofessional
- **Busy patterns** – Pull focus away from facial expression
- **Pure white** – Causes glare and washes out skin tone

👉 **Better alternatives:** charcoal instead of black, cream instead of white

💡 **Why this matters**
Color affects perceived authority and warmth before facial features are even processed.

---

## 👕 Fit and Style Guidelines

### What Proper Fit Looks Like
- Shoulders align with natural shoulder line
- Chest feels comfortable without pulling
- Sleeves end at wrist bone
- No excess fabric bunching or sagging
- Neckline lies flat without gaping

💡 **Why this matters**
Ill-fitting clothing instantly lowers perceived professionalism—even in high-quality photos.

---

## 👔 Necklines and Framing

### Best Necklines for Headshots
- **Crew neck** – Classic, professional, universally safe
- **V-neck** – Elongates face and neck
- **Boat neck** – Adds width, good for narrow shoulders
- **Open collar** – Approachable, modern, tech-friendly
- **Turtleneck** – Polished but can feel formal or rigid

---

## 🧥 Blazers and Jackets

### When to Wear a Blazer
Recommended for:
- Corporate & finance roles
- Law, government, executive positions
- When projecting authority and leadership

**Blazer Tips**
- Choose a well-fitted blazer that doesn't pull at buttons
- Neutral colors (navy, charcoal) work best
- Leaving it unbuttoned feels more relaxed
- Match fabric tone to your skin undertone

💡 **Why this matters**
A blazer instantly elevates perceived seniority and credibility.

---

## 🏢 Industry-Specific Outfit Guide

**Corporate & Finance**
✔ Navy or charcoal blazer
✔ Solid white or light blue shirt
✖ Casual fabrics, bold colors

**Creative Industries**
✔ Jewel tones or subtle textures
✔ Relaxed but intentional fit
✖ Overly rigid corporate suits

**Tech & Startups**
✔ Open collar or light sweater
✔ Neutral colors
✖ Ties or overly formal attire

**Healthcare & Education**
✔ Warm, approachable colors
✔ Soft fabrics
✖ Stark contrasts or dark palettes

**Sales & Client-Facing Roles**
✔ Blazer + warm-toned shirt
✔ Balance authority and friendliness

---

## ⚠️ Avoid These Common Mistakes

- Tank tops – too casual
- T-shirts – informal outside creative roles
- Visible logos or branding – distracting
- Wrinkled clothing – signals carelessness
- Stains or damage – always double-check

---

## 🗓️ Day-of Preparation Checklist
- Try outfit the night before
- Ensure comfort and mobility
- Steam or iron clothing
- Check fit under natural light
